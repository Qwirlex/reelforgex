Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 21, 2026

ReelForgeX (“the App”) is a desktop application for Windows that helps a user turn their own long videos into short vertical clips that follow a brand brief, and publish those clips to the user’s own social media accounts. This policy explains what data the App handles and where it goes.

1. The App runs on your computer

The developer of ReelForgeX does not operate servers that receive your data. There is no ReelForgeX account, no analytics and no tracking. Projects, videos, rendered clips, settings, API keys and sign-in tokens are stored only on your computer. API keys and tokens are encrypted with the Windows Data Protection API (DPAPI) and are available only to your Windows user account.

2. Services you choose to connect

The App works with third-party services only after you connect them with your own credentials. Data is sent directly from your computer to these services, under their own terms and privacy policies:

  • Google Gemini (through Google AI Studio or your own Google Cloud / Vertex AI project) — the text of the brief you load, speech transcripts, short audio segments and sampled video frames are sent for analysis: to understand the brief, recognize speech, pick moments and check clips. Google Privacy Policy.
  • YouTube (YouTube Data API) — when you connect a channel, the App receives an access token to read your channel name and ID, upload the clips you choose and read view counts of videos it uploaded. YouTube Terms of Service, Google Privacy Policy.
  • Google Drive (optional) — only to download a watermark image from a folder you specify.
  • TikTok (Login Kit and Content Posting API) — when you connect a TikTok account, TikTok issues tokens limited to the permissions you approve: basic profile information (display name and avatar), video upload and video publishing. The App uses them only to show which account is connected and to publish a video when you explicitly press Publish, with the visibility and settings you select. TikTok Privacy Policy.
  • OpenAI (optional) — if you select OpenAI for speech recognition, audio segments are sent to OpenAI for transcription.

3. What the App does not do

  • It never sees or stores your passwords: sign-in happens on the service’s own web page in your browser.
  • It does not post, delete or change anything on your accounts without your explicit action.
  • It does not read your messages, followers, likes or other account data beyond what is listed above.
  • It does not sell, share or transfer your data to the developer or to advertisers.

4. Update checks

To tell you about new versions, the App requests public release information from GitHub (api.github.com). No personal data is sent; GitHub may see your IP address as with any web request.

5. Google API Services

ReelForgeX’s use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

6. Keeping and deleting your data

Your data stays on your computer until you delete it. You can disconnect any account in the App at any time; this removes its tokens from your computer. Uninstalling the App and deleting its folders (%APPDATA%\ReelForgeX and your projects folder) removes everything. You can also revoke the App’s access in your Google account security settings and in TikTok under Settings → Security → Manage app permissions.

7. Children

The App is not directed to children under 13 (or the minimum age required in your country) and does not knowingly process their data.

8. Changes

We may update this policy. The date at the top shows the latest version.

9. Contact

Questions about privacy: open an issue at github.com/Qwirlex/reelforgex/issues.
